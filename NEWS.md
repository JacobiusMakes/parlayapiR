# parlayapiR 0.1.0

Initial release.

- Request functions: `pa_sports()`, `pa_odds()`, `pa_props()`,
  `pa_historical()`, `pa_closing_odds()`.
- Local math: `pa_devig()` (multiplicative, additive, power),
  `pa_kelly()`, and odds conversion helpers.
- `pa_auth()` / `PARLAY_API_KEY` authentication and a keyless
  `pa_sandbox()` mode.
- Vignette: "Line shopping and devigging in R".
